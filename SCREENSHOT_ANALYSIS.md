# Bump'em Screenshot Analysis — Design Spec for Physical Edition

This document captures layout, colors, typography, and components from the in-game screenshots so the printable game can match the original as closely as possible.

---

## 1. Board (board.png)

### Overall structure
- **Layout:** Cross-shaped track with four distinct player quadrants.
- **Background:** Dark green with a subtle interwoven fabric/mesh texture.
- **Outer border:** Thick frame with a gradient: red → orange → yellow → green → light blue → purple (neon/glow effect).
- **Track:** White track with grey borders; central path connects the four quadrants.

### Player quadrants (clockwise from top)
| Position | Color  | Character   | Name          |
|----------|--------|-------------|---------------|
| Top      | Red    | Man, dark hair, black jacket | Mihuil       |
| Right    | Green  | Parrot with pirate hat       | Capt. Scurvy |
| Bottom   | Light blue | Grey alien, large eyes   | Roswell      |
| Bottom-right | Yellow | Man, grey hair, brown jacket | Jack     |

### Per-quadrant elements
- **Character portrait** in a square frame.
- **Path** in that player’s color (red / green / light blue / yellow).
- **Labels:** "START", "FINISH" (checkered flag).
- **Direction:** Small white triangular arrows along the path.
- **Special spaces:** "Oil Slick" on the outer curve of each quadrant; "TURBO" on the straight connecting segments between quadrants.
- **Tokens:** Four small oval tokens per player (color-matched) below the portrait.
- **Icons on path:** Smiley/heart and pig icons in player color (decorative).

### Control panel (left, for reference only — not on printable board)
- Grey/white gradient, light blue at bottom; same mesh texture.
- Large circular red “PUSH FOR TICKET” button (glossy, metallic, silver rim, white bold sans-serif).
- Dark grey horizontal ticket slot below button.

### Replication notes
- **Track:** White fill, grey stroke; clear segment boundaries.
- **Typography:** Simple sans-serif for START, FINISH, Oil Slick, TURBO.
- **Direction:** White triangles (arrow shape) along the track.
- **Colors:** Saturated red, green, light blue, yellow for the four paths; rainbow gradient on outer board border.
- **No character names on board:** Do not print character names (Mihuil, Capt. Scurvy, Roswell, Jack) on the printable board—use quadrants/colors only.

### Game logo (bumper cars — best seen on BUMP'EM card)
- **Where it appears:** Most clearly on the BUMP'EM card (ticket-bumpem.png): the central illustration.
- **Description:** Two stylized bumper cars — rounded, flattened, cartoonish; white or light grey; positioned one slightly behind the other; with a white, fluid “splatter” or melting shape at the base where they meet. This graphic is the main game logo / brand mark.
- **Use in print:** Include this bumper-cars illustration on the BUMP'EM card when replicating it. It can also be reused as the game logo on the rule sheet, box, or other materials if desired.

### Car tokens (for printable standees)
- **Count:** 16 cars total — 4 per player color.
- **Colors (match quadrants):** Red, green, light blue, yellow.
- **In-game appearance:** Small oval tokens/markers, color-matched to each quadrant; shown below character portrait in each quadrant.
- **Printable spec:** Provide 16 car models (e.g. fold-and-stand standees or punch-out tokens): 4 identical red, 4 identical green, 4 identical light blue, 4 identical yellow. Same car shape for all; color differentiates players. Size to fit pit-row and track spaces.

## 2. Numbered ticket cards (1–12)

### Shared card system
- **Orientation:** Portrait (taller than wide); standard playing-card proportion.
- **Corners:** Rounded.
- **Border:** Multi-layer:
  - Outer: textured, braided-rope or metallic gold/bronze trim (some cards: thin dark line then thicker gold/tan/beige).
  - Inner: thin dark line, then pale yellow-tan/beige band.
  - Some variation: double border (inner thin dark, outer thicker light yellow-brown/beige); occasionally dotted/dashed inner line.
- **Top strip (UI element in screenshot):** Narrow horizontal bar, dark purple/blue, metallic/glossy; sometimes with purple/green/blue accents. **Optional for print:** can be omitted or shown as a thin decorative bar.
- **Card background:** Off-white / cream / very pale yellow; some cards have a slight yellow-green tint. Subtle texture throughout:
  - Diagonal herringbone or chevron.
  - Fine linen, canvas, or speckled paper.
  - Slight distressed / aged look.
- **Main number:** Large, bold, black or dark grey, sans-serif, centered in upper half; primary identifier.
- **Background graphic (behind number):** Faint, semi-transparent, purple-pink or light grey; thematic (race track, car, trophy, checkered flag, etc.); halftone/dot or line-art; never obscures number or text.
- **Instruction text:** Lower half of card:
  - **Color:** Dark green (ticket 1), dark grey/black (most others), dark olive/forest green (ticket 12).
  - **Font:** Clear sans-serif; line breaks as on card.
  - **Emphasis:** Bold for numbers-in-words, “OR”, “AND”, and key actions (e.g. “one space forward”, “take another move ticket”, “switch one of your cars”, “pit row”, “bump”).

### Per-card wording and emphasis (from screenshots)

| # | Wording | Bold / emphasis |
|---|--------|------------------|
| 1 | Move a car from pit row to your start space **OR** move a car on the track one space forward. | Move, OR, one space forward |
| 2 | Move a car on the track **two** spaces forward. | two |
| 3 | Move a car on the track **three spaces forward** **AND** take another **move ticket.** | three spaces forward, AND, move ticket. |
| 4 | Move a car on the track **four** spaces forward. | four |
| 5 | Move a car on the track **five** spaces forward. | five (or “five spaces forward”) |
| 6 | Move a car on the track **six spaces forward** **OR** **nine spaces backward.** | six spaces forward, OR, nine spaces backward. |
| 7 | Move a car on the track **seven spaces forward** **OR** **switch one of your cars** with another player's car. | seven spaces forward, OR, switch one of your cars |
| 8 | Move a car on the track **eight spaces forward** **OR** split the move **between two cars.** | eight spaces forward, OR, between two cars. (One card had “OR” in lime green.) |
| 9 | Move a car on the track **nine spaces forward** **OR** **six spaces backward.** | nine spaces forward, OR, six spaces backward. |
| 10 | Move a car on the track ten spaces forward. | Can bold “ten” for consistency. |
| 11 | Move a car on the track **eleven** spaces forward **OR** one space backward. | eleven, OR |
| 12 | Move a car from pit row to your start space **OR** move a car on the track **twelve spaces forward.** | OR, twelve spaces forward. |

### Color palette (number tickets)
- **Card face:** Off-white / cream / pale yellow (#F5F5DC–#FFF8E7 range); optional slight yellow-green.
- **Border:** Gold/bronze/tan/beige (#C4A35A–#D2B48C); inner dark line (#2C1810–#3D2314).
- **Number & body text:** Dark grey / black (#1A1A1A–#2D2D2D); ticket 1 & 12: dark green / olive (#2D4A2D–#3D5C3D).
- **Accent (optional):** “OR” in lime green on one card (#8BC34A range); else same as body text, bold.
- **Background graphic:** Muted purple-pink or light grey, very low opacity (~10–15%).

## 3. BUMP'EM card (ticket-bumpem.png)

### Layout and style
- **Orientation:** Portrait; same card proportion as number tickets.
- **Style:** Retro halftone; comic/poster feel; distinct from number tickets.
- **Background:** Light pinkish-purple with subtle distressed texture; uniform halftone dot pattern over entire card.
- **Border:** Thin dark (black or dark purple).
- **Shadow:** Soft drop shadow (present in screenshot; optional for print).

### Content (top to bottom)
1. **Main title:** “BUMP 'EM!” — large, bold, cartoonish yellow with thick dark purple outline; angled slightly up left-to-right.
2. **Illustration (game logo):** Two stylized bumper cars — rounded, flattened, cartoonish; white/light grey; one slightly behind the other; with a white fluid “splatter” or melting shape at the base. Beneath them, abstract light purple “puddle” or “splash” shapes on white. This is the main game logo (best seen on this card).
3. **Card title:** “BUMP 'EM!” again — large, bold, uppercase dark purple, centered.
4. **Instruction:** “Take a car from your **pit row** and **bump** another player's car.” — smaller sans-serif, dark purple; “pit row” and “bump” bold.

### BUMP'EM color palette
- **Background:** Light pinkish-purple (#E6D5E6–#E8D8E8).
- **Title (yellow):** #FFD54F–#FFEB3B with dark purple outline (#4A148C–#6A1B9A).
- **Body text & second title:** Dark purple (#4A148C–#5E35B1).
- **Bumper cars:** Light grey / off-white (#E0E0E0–#F5F5F5).
- **Splash/puddle:** Light purple (#B39DDB–#CE93D8).
- **Halftone:** Dark purple or grey at low density.

## 4. Summary: What to match for “as close as possible”

### Board
- Cross layout; 4 quadrants; red / green / light blue / yellow paths.
- White track, grey borders; START, FINISH, Oil Slick, TURBO in correct positions.
- Directional white triangles; rainbow gradient on outer board border.
- Dark green textured background.

### Number tickets (1–12)
- Portrait card proportion; rounded corners; layered gold/tan border and off-white/cream face.
- One large bold number per card; faint racing-themed graphic behind number.
- Exact wording and bolding as in table above; dark grey or dark green text; optional “OR” accent color.
- Subtle card texture (herringbone/linen/speckle).

### BUMP'EM
- Halftone, yellow “BUMP 'EM!” with purple outline, bumper-car art, purple instruction text with “pit row” and “bump” bold.

### Assets to create or reuse
- **Board:** Vector or high-res track segments, arrows, labels; gradient border; texture overlay.
- **Tickets:** Card template (border, background, texture); 12 number + 1 BUMP'EM face art; type set in a clean sans-serif (e.g. Arial, Helvetica, or similar for body; bolder weight for numbers).
- **Rule sheet:** Pull wording from PROJECT_SUMMARY.md; keep typography clear and consistent with card text.

Use this document as the single source of truth when generating the production-ready printable PDF (board, ticket sheets, rule sheet) so the result stays as close as possible to the screenshots.
